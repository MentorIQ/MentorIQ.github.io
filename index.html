Welcome to MentorIQ

A free, independent platform dedicated to clear thinking and reliable information. Learn about the world around you—no exams, no pressure, just education.

What is MentorIQ?

MentorIQ is an open educational resource built for people who want to understand the world better. We curate and explain topics in science, history, society, technology, and everyday life. Our content is written for genuine learners—not test takers—with accuracy and clarity as our core principles.

We believe everyone deserves access to trustworthy information, explained in plain language.

What We Cover

Explore topics across multiple disciplines:

  • History & Culture: Events, movements, civilizations, and cultural heritage that shaped humanity
  • Polity & Politics: Political systems, structures, and principles of governance
  • Government Schemes and Governance: Public policies, administrative systems, and social welfare programs
  • Current Affairs: Recent events, news, and contemporary global issues
  • Economy: Economic principles, markets, trade, and financial systems
  • International Relations: Global politics, diplomacy, and international cooperation
  • Geography: Physical and human geography, maps, and spatial understanding
  • Environment: Environmental systems, ecology, and sustainability
  • Science & Tech: How science and technology work and their impact on society
  • Educational Awareness: Learning resources, skills, and knowledge for lifelong education
